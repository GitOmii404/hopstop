Setenil de las Bodegas
Cadiz, Spain • 💎 9.8
💎 9.8 Underrated Score • Spain
Setenil de las Bodegas
📍 Cadiz, Andalusia, Spain
An ancient whitewashed village sculpted directly under colossal 50-ton natural river gorge boulders. Pure Andalusian silence and cave cafes.
🤫 Traveler Secret: Walk into Cuevas del Sol at sunset for local wine inside the bedrock.
Blagaj Tekke
Mostar, Bosnia • 💎 9.9
💎 9.9 Underrated Score • Bosnia
Blagaj Tekke
📍 Mostar Basin, Bosnia & Herzegovina
A 600-year-old Ottoman Dervish monastery perched dramatically at the mouth of an icy emerald karst spring cascading from a 200m vertical cliff.
🤫 Traveler Secret: Order Bosnian mountain tea on the wooden stilts over the spring.
Huacachina Oasis
Ica, Peru • 💎 9.7
💎 9.7 Underrated Score • Peru
Huacachina Oasis
📍 Ica Desert, Peru
A surreal sapphire lagoon surrounded by towering palm trees nestled deep within South America's most gigantic golden rolling sand dunes.
🤫 Traveler Secret: Hike to the northern ridge at 6:15 PM for zero tourists and golden dune shadows.
Giethoorn Village
Overijssel, Netherlands • 💎 9.6
💎 9.6 Underrated Score • Netherlands
Giethoorn Village
📍 Overijssel, Netherlands
A completely car-free fairy-tale settlement with 176 hand-built wooden bridges and electric whisper boats sliding through reflective canals.
🤫 Traveler Secret: Rent an electric punter boat before 9 AM when morning mist hangs over the canals.
Svaneti Fortresses
Ushguli, Georgia • 💎 9.8
💎 9.8 Underrated Score • Georgia
Svaneti Fortresses
📍 Ushguli, Caucasus, Georgia
Glacial stone defense towers dating to the 9th century, guarded by the towering 5,200m icy peaks of Mount Shkhara. Pure off-grid wilderness.
🤫 Traveler Secret: Stay in a stone family guesthouse to taste homemade Kubdari pie.
Chefchaouen Medina
Rif, Morocco • 💎 9.5
💎 9.5 Underrated Score • Morocco
Chefchaouen Blue Medina
📍 Rif Mountains, Morocco
An ancient mountain city painted in 50 shades of celestial electric cobalt, indigo, and powder blue against rugged mountain peaks.
🤫 Traveler Secret: Climb up to the Spanish Mosque at twilight for the blue glow over the valley.
Meteora Monasteries
Thessaly, Greece • 💎 9.9
💎 9.9 Underrated Score • Greece
Meteora Monasteries
📍 Thessaly, Greece
Centuries-old Eastern Orthodox monasteries miraculously perched on top of colossal natural sandstone rock pillars rising 400 meters into the sky.
🤫 Traveler Secret: Hike the hidden monk trail behind Holy Trinity Monastery.
